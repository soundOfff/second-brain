# Recap — Don't Use AI to Write Things That You Present as Your Own Work

A short opinion post by testing consultant [[entities/james-bach]] (Satisfice) arguing that people should never let AI draft text they then present as their own work, and should not even claim AI merely "helped" — because that framing collapses in practice into AI doing the writing wholesale [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo]. Bach states his personal policy: he never lets AI draft anything with his name on it, contrasting this with taking a helicopter to the summit of Everest and calling it a climb [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo].

He illustrates the ethical line with two personal anecdotes: ghostwriting an article entirely himself for a poor writer who took no active role (which still bears the other person's name, something Bach says still bothers him), versus a genuinely collaborative chapter with Jeff Nadelman, co-credited, built from Nadelman's notes and many hours of conversation [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo]. His argument (opinion) is that AI-assisted writing is unverifiable in the way human collaboration is — he can confirm a co-author's contribution by asking them, but cannot verify someone's claim that AI only "helped" — so any use of AI to write, if undisclosed as authorship, amounts to lying about who did the work [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo]. He concludes that anyone who says they use AI to write should be treated as likely using it for all their writing ("full laziness"), and states he will treat such work as slop/spam and expects the "community of serious professionals" to discount it [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo].

## Key claims

- Bach's stated personal policy: no AI drafts anything published under his name, not even one sentence [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo].
- Bach (opinion): claiming AI only "helped" is functionally indistinguishable from AI doing the writing, and is unverifiable to a reader [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo].
- Bach (opinion): presenting AI-drafted work as one's own is a lie; he will treat such work as slop/spam [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo].
- Bach distinguishes his own ghostwriting (author took no active role, fully Bach's work under someone else's name) from genuine co-authorship with Jeff Nadelman (built on Nadelman's notes and extensive conversation) [2026-06-24-don-t-use-ai-to-write-things-that-you-present-as-your-own-wo].

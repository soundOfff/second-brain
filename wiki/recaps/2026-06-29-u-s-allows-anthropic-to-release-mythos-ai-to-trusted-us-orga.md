# Recap — U.S. allows Anthropic to release Mythos AI to 'trusted' US organizations

Semafor (Reed Albergotti and Ben Smith) reports that on Friday, June 26, 2026, the U.S.
government lifted its block on **[[entities/anthropic]]**'s **[[entities/mythos]]**
model (Claude Mythos 5), letting Anthropic release it to more than 100 U.S.
institutions — major companies and government agencies — named in an annex to a letter
from Commerce Secretary **[[entities/howard-lutnick]]** to Anthropic's chief compute
officer **[[entities/tom-brown]]** [2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].
Lutnick wrote that "appropriate safeguards are in place to permit certain trusted
partners to access the Claude Mythos 5 Model," citing "significant progress" from daily
talks since export controls were imposed roughly two weeks earlier — controls that had
followed warnings from Amazon and other companies that Mythos and its weaker sibling
**[[entities/fable-5]]** could be "jailbroken" for malicious use
[2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga]. The letter
does not address Fable 5, though people close to the talks said the government is
moving toward releasing it too, on an unclear timeline
[2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].

The same day, **[[entities/openai]]** released its own latest model, **GPT-5.6**, to a
short list of government-approved partners
[2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga]. The article
frames Lutnick's letter as the start of a new U.S. regulatory regime giving the
government direct control over frontier model releases, and notes frustration among
European officials and other allies left in the dark about when they'll get access
[2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga]. Per earlier
Semafor reporting cited in the piece, the U.S. had specifically flagged concern that
Mythos had been released to partners too closely linked to China — reportedly referring
to a South Korean telecommunications provider
[2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].

## Key claims

- The U.S. government lifted its Mythos export block for a named list of 100+ trusted
  U.S. institutions on Friday, June 26, 2026, per a letter from Commerce Secretary
  Howard Lutnick to Anthropic's Tom Brown
  [2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].
- Fable 5 was not addressed in the release letter; sources say a release is likely but
  unscheduled [2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].
- The original export controls, imposed roughly two weeks prior, followed warnings
  from Amazon and others that Mythos/Fable 5 could be jailbroken for malicious use
  [2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].
- OpenAI released GPT-5.6 to a short list of government-approved partners the same day
  as the Mythos decision [2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].
- The U.S. had reportedly flagged concern that Mythos reached a partner too closely
  linked to China (a South Korean telecom provider), per earlier Semafor reporting
  [2026-06-29-u-s-allows-anthropic-to-release-mythos-ai-to-trusted-us-orga].

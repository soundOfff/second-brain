# Recap — Nano Banana 2 Lite

Short link-blog post by **[[entities/simon-willison]]** noting the release of **Nano
Banana 2 Lite**, also called **Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image**
(`gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image` in the API) — described by **[[entities/google]]** as its
"fastest and cheapest [[entities/gemini]] image model, engineered for velocity and
scale" [2026-07-01-nano-banana-2-lite]. Willison tested it in AI Studio with a
"Where's Waldo style image but it's where is the raccoon holding a ham radio" prompt,
and says he liked the result better than earlier **[[entities/nano-banana]]** models he
tried in April, though it misspelled "Forest Festival" two different ways in the image
[2026-07-01-nano-banana-2-lite].

## Key claims

- Nano Banana 2 Lite = Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`),
  positioned by Google as its fastest/cheapest Gemini image model
  [2026-07-01-nano-banana-2-lite].
- Willison preferred its output on a "Where's Waldo" style test prompt over earlier Nano
  Banana models he'd tried in April [2026-07-01-nano-banana-2-lite].
- The model misspelled in-image text ("Forest Festival") two different ways in his test
  [2026-07-01-nano-banana-2-lite].

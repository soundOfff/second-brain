# Recap — Ford hired AI and sacked humans. It backfired badly

The Independent reports that **[[entities/ford]]** has rehired over 350 veteran
engineers — internally nicknamed "gray beards" — over the past three years to fix
problems caused by the company's aggressive AI-driven quality-inspection automation,
after the automation issues cost Ford billions of dollars (per Bloomberg, cited in the
article) [2026-06-29-ford-hired-ai-and-sacked-humans-it-backfired-badly]. Ford COO
**[[entities/kumar-galhotra]]** is quoted: "We had been relying more and more on
automated quality systems and not getting the desired results ... We brought back
technical specialists and they hunt for failure points before a part ever reaches the
plant floor" [2026-06-29-ford-hired-ai-and-sacked-humans-it-backfired-badly]. The
article states Ford had leaned on AI-driven inspection systems to streamline production
and address quality control, but the company acknowledged the AI lacked nuanced
judgment for complex problems [2026-06-29-ford-hired-ai-and-sacked-humans-it-backfired-badly].

After rehiring the engineers, Ford's quality reportedly improved markedly: per the
latest J.D. Power Initial Quality Survey, Ford ranked top among mainstream brands for
the first time in 16 years [2026-06-29-ford-hired-ai-and-sacked-humans-it-backfired-badly].
Ford still has quality issues with older vehicles and remains the most-recalled
automaker in the US, which executives attribute to past automation-related issues rather
than the human rehiring [2026-06-29-ford-hired-ai-and-sacked-humans-it-backfired-badly].
Ford says it will not abandon AI but will now pair it with human oversight and
experience. **[[entities/charles-poon]]**, Ford VP of vehicle hardware engineering, is
quoted: "Artificial intelligence is a fantastic tool, but it's only as good as the
information you use to train it ... Mistakenly, we thought that by just introducing
artificial intelligence and ingesting the design requirements that we had, that that
would produce a high-quality product"
[2026-06-29-ford-hired-ai-and-sacked-humans-it-backfired-badly].

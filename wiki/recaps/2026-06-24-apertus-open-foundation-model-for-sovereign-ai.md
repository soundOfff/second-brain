# Recap — Apertus: Open Foundation Model for Sovereign AI

Marketing/landing page for **[[entities/apertus]]**, a foundation language model developed by the
**[[entities/swiss-ai-initiative]]**, described as a collaborative effort between
**[[entities/epfl]]**, **[[entities/eth-zurich]]**, and **[[entities/cscs]]**
[2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai]. The page positions Apertus as "fully
open" — training data, code, weights, methods, and alignment principles all documented and
reproducible — with the tagline "Apertus is to AI as Open is to Source," and frames it around
**[[concepts/sovereign-ai]]** and EU AI Act compliance
[2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].

Per the page, Apertus is built to meet **[[concepts/eu-ai-act]]** requirements — respecting
opt-outs, removing PII, and preventing memorization — and is offered at two parameter scales, 8B
and 70B, described as competitive with top open models at equivalent scale
[2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai]. It is described as multilingual "from
day one," trained on 1000+ languages [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
**[[entities/swisscom]]** is named as a "Strategic Partner" of the Swiss AI Initiative
[2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai]. A news timeline on the page lists:
"Apertus Mini" (Jun 15) — 16 small language models released to demonstrate distillation and
quantization techniques; an Apertus technical report accepted at ACL 2026 (Apr 25 note); and
"Apertus for Ticino" (Mar 17) — a fine-tuned model powering in-house AI translation
[2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].

## Key claims

- Apertus is developed by the Swiss AI Initiative, a collaboration between EPFL, ETH Zurich, and
  CSCS [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
- The project claims full openness of training data, code, weights, methods, and alignment
  principles [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
- Apertus is built to meet EU AI Act requirements: opt-out respect, PII removal, and memorization
  prevention [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
- Apertus ships at 8B and 70B parameter scales and is claimed to be competitive with top open
  models at equivalent scale [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
- The model is multilingual from initial release, trained on 1000+ languages
  [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
- Swisscom is named as a Strategic Partner of the Swiss AI Initiative
  [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
- "Apertus Mini" is a set of 16 small language models demonstrating distillation/quantization
  techniques [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].
- A fine-tuned Apertus variant ("Apertus for Ticino") powers in-house AI translation
  [2026-06-24-apertus-open-foundation-model-for-sovereign-ai].

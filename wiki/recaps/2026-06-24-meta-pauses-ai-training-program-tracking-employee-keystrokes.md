# Recap — Meta pauses AI training program tracking employee keystrokes after internal leak

**[[entities/business-insider]]** (reporters **[[entities/charles-rollet]]** and
**[[entities/pranav-dixit]]**) reports that **[[entities/meta]]** paused an internal AI
training program after sensitive data collected under it was accessible company-wide,
per screenshots obtained by the outlet
[2026-06-24-meta-pauses-ai-training-program-tracking-employee-keystrokes]. The exposed
data reportedly included employees' private conversations, performance data, and
transcriptions; the incident was internally classified SEV 2 on a 0–5 severity scale
where 0 is most severe [2026-06-24-meta-pauses-ai-training-program-tracking-employee-keystrokes].
A Meta spokesperson confirmed the incident, said there was "no indication ... that any
data was improperly accessed by Meta employees," and that the program is paused pending
investigation [2026-06-24-meta-pauses-ai-training-program-tracking-employee-keystrokes].

The program, called the **Model Capability Initiative (MCI)**, was announced by Meta in
April and is mandatory for most staff; it uses employees' keystrokes and mouse movements
as AI training data, and had already drawn internal backlash over discomfort with the
data collection before this leak [2026-06-24-meta-pauses-ai-training-program-tracking-employee-keystrokes].
Internal reaction to the leak itself was described as angry, with one employee quoted:
"I am incensed" and "the fact that this data wasn't locked down as originally promised
is super frustrating" [2026-06-24-meta-pauses-ai-training-program-tracking-employee-keystrokes].
The article frames this as part of a string of recent Meta security incidents, also
citing an AI chatbot flaw that allowed hijacking of multiple Instagram accounts, and
(per prior reporting from The Information) a "rogue AI agent" that caused a severe
incident in March [2026-06-24-meta-pauses-ai-training-program-tracking-employee-keystrokes].

# Recap — The AI industry is pouring millions into US elections

A launch post by Brian Merchant for a new *Blood in the Machine* podcast/show, whose pilot episode covers AI-industry political spending [2026-06-29-the-ai-industry-is-pouring-millions-into-us-elections]. The source itself is mostly a meta-post about the show's launch (credits, format, motivation) rather than the substantive reporting; the actual content is described as an interview with **[[entities/molly-white]]** (technologist/journalist behind the *Citation Needed* newsletter) about "the ballooning influence of AI and crypto money in politics" and her new tracking project, **Tech Influence Watch**, which monitors hundreds of millions of dollars spent by AI and crypto companies to influence U.S. elections [2026-06-29-the-ai-industry-is-pouring-millions-into-us-elections]. The episode also reportedly covers resistance to a nuclear-powered AI data center and an unspecified "A24's Google debacle" [2026-06-29-the-ai-industry-is-pouring-millions-into-us-elections].

Merchant frames the show as a counter-programming effort against business-oriented tech podcasts, aiming instead to document worker organizing, data-center protests, and public backlash against AI — which he characterizes as "the Summer of Ludd" and "the open booing of AI at college graduations" [2026-06-29-the-ai-industry-is-pouring-millions-into-us-elections]. Note this recap is necessarily thin: the captured source text is the show-announcement post, not a transcript of the episode's actual claims/figures about election spending.

## Key claims

- Molly White (Citation Needed) launched a project called Tech Influence Watch to track AI and crypto industry election spending in the US, described as "hundreds of millions of dollars" [2026-06-29-the-ai-industry-is-pouring-millions-into-us-elections].
- Brian Merchant (opinion/framing) characterizes 2026 as a period of growing organized resistance to AI — data center protests, workplace organizing, and public "booing" of AI at college graduations, which he dubs "the Summer of Ludd" [2026-06-29-the-ai-industry-is-pouring-millions-into-us-elections].
- The podcast pilot also touches on resistance to a nuclear-powered AI data center and a dispute involving A24 and Google, neither of which is detailed in this source [2026-06-29-the-ai-industry-is-pouring-millions-into-us-elections].

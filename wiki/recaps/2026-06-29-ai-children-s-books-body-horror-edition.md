# Recap — AI children's books, body horror edition

**[[entities/lcamtuf]]**, following up on an earlier post cataloguing ~220
AI-generated children's books and arguing they were all stylistically the same, bought
one of the Amazon-bestselling AI-generated children's encyclopedias to check quality
rather than just sameness [2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition]. He
speculates children's encyclopedias are disproportionately targeted by AI-content
publishers because they sell well, the buyer (often a gift-giving relative) isn't the
reader/judge of quality, and — unlike fiction — they don't risk infringing anyone's
guarded IP [2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition].

The post is largely visual: he shows illustrations from the book he bought (which he
says is the #1 bestseller in its category on Amazon) depicting AI image-generation
artifacts he characterizes as "body horror" — a reflection reaching out, animals and
trees fused into pulsating masses, a cat that seems altered, disembodied
voices/tendrils — and argues, sardonically, that this is what's currently being sold to
and shaping the minds of young children
[2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition]. His conclusion is that future
models may produce flawless output, but the current generation of AI-generated
children's books is actively low-quality/disturbing and reaching real sales volume
[2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition]. This is the author's opinion and
observation from a single purchased example, not a systematic study.

## Key claims

- The author previously catalogued roughly 220 AI-generated children's books and
  argued they were visually/stylistically interchangeable
  [2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition].
- He purchased a #1-category-bestseller AI-generated children's encyclopedia on Amazon
  and found illustrations with severe body-horror-style AI generation artifacts
  (fused limbs/creatures, malformed anatomy) [2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition].
- He argues the artwork points to a "flagship model from a major US-based lab," though
  he does not name the specific model [2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition].
- His view: rankings/reviews can be gamed, but the prevalence of this content across
  Amazon's children's book categories suggests real sales volume
  [2026-06-29-ai-children-s-books-body-horror-edition] (author's opinion).

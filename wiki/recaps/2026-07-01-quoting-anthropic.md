# Recap — Quoting Anthropic

A short "quotation" post on Simon Willison's blog (dated 30 June 2026) reproduces a tweet
from **[[entities/anthropic]]**: "We've received notice that the Department of Commerce
has lifted export controls on Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5. We'll begin restoring access
tomorrow, and will share an update soon." [2026-07-01-quoting-anthropic]. This implies
these Claude models had previously been under US Department of Commerce export
controls restricting access in some regions [2026-07-01-quoting-anthropic].

## Key claims

- The US Department of Commerce lifted export controls on Anthropic's Claude Fable 5
  and Claude Mythos 5 models, per Anthropic's own tweet, dated 30 June 2026
  [2026-07-01-quoting-anthropic].
- Anthropic said it would begin restoring access "tomorrow" (i.e., 1 July 2026) with a
  further update to follow [2026-07-01-quoting-anthropic].

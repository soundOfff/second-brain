# Recap — A Survey of Automated Presentation Coaching: Systems, Methods, and Open Challenges

Captured page is the arXiv abstract/landing page (arXiv:2606.27380, submitted 11 May 2026) for a
survey paper, not the full paper text, by Wen Liang, Li Siyan, Zackary Rackauckas, and
**[[entities/julia-hirschberg]]**, accepted into the BEA 2026 workshop at ACL
[2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods]. Per the abstract, the
survey covers [[concepts/automated-presentation-coaching]] as a field sitting at the intersection
of [[concepts/computer-assisted-pronunciation-training]] (CAPT), [[concepts/prosody-modeling]],
and [[concepts/text-to-speech]], and claims to be the first systematic survey/comparison of such
systems along these dimensions
[2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].

The abstract states the survey reviews and categorizes systems spanning pronunciation tutors,
fluency/prosody coaches, multimodal trainers, and conference Q&A practice tools, and introduces a
five-dimensional task taxonomy — segmental pronunciation, lexical stress, suprasegmental prosody,
pacing, and content faithfulness — explicitly mapping surveyed systems onto it to expose coverage
gaps [2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods]. It also reviews
core technical methods, namely TTS-based exemplar generation and diagnostic methods for
pronunciation, prosody, and fluency assessment
[2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods]. Cited open challenges:
scarcity of annotated presentation corpora, achieving accent-fair feedback across diverse L1
(native-language) backgrounds, and delivering low-latency diagnostics for real-time rehearsal
[2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].

## Key claims

- The survey claims to be the first to systematically survey and compare automated presentation
  coaching systems across CAPT, prosody modeling, and speech synthesis
  [2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].
- It introduces a five-dimensional task taxonomy: segmental pronunciation, lexical stress,
  suprasegmental prosody, pacing, and content faithfulness
  [2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].
- Surveyed system categories include pronunciation tutors, fluency/prosody coaches, multimodal
  trainers, and conference Q&A practice tools
  [2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].
- Core technical methods reviewed: TTS-based exemplar generation, and diagnostic methods for
  pronunciation, prosody, and fluency assessment
  [2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].
- Named open challenges: scarce annotated presentation corpora, accent-fair feedback across L1
  backgrounds, and low-latency real-time diagnostics
  [2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].
- Accepted into the BEA 2026 workshop at ACL [2026-06-29-a-survey-of-automated-presentation-coaching-systems-methods].

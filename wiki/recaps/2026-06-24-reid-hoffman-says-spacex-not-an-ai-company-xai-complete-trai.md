# Recap — Reid Hoffman says SpaceX 'not an AI company', xAI 'complete train wreck'

A Fortune article (by Nick Lichtenberg) summarizing remarks **[[entities/reid-hoffman]]** made on Rana el Kaliouby's *Pioneers of AI* podcast [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai]. Hoffman — a founder, investor in both **[[entities/openai]]** and **[[entities/anthropic]]**, and outgoing Microsoft board member — argues **[[entities/spacex]]** is not really an AI company despite AI being central to its June 12 IPO narrative and its subsequent acquisition of **[[entities/cursor]]**; he likens the strategy to Barry Diller's IAC roll-up model, "buy your way into relevance," and dismisses SpaceX's AI-infrastructure leasing business (including to Anthropic) as being "a premium-priced CoreWeave" [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai]. He separately calls **[[entities/xai]]** "a complete train wreck," citing the departure of all 11 original xAI cofounders by May 2026 (beginning with Tony Wu in February) and Grok's lagging benchmark performance [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].

On the U.S. government's June 11 export-control order forcing Anthropic to pull its **[[entities/fable]]** and **[[entities/mythos]]** models from foreign markets — reportedly triggered by Amazon CEO Andy Jassy flagging a jailbreak in Fable 5 that Anthropic itself was already working to fix — Hoffman calls the government's response "autocratic willy-nilly," criticizing the lack of a principled rationale and the asymmetry of Anthropic being penalized while OpenAI was not [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai]. He pushes back on framing OpenAI vs. Anthropic as zero-sum, sketching Anthropic as strong in code/design/legal and OpenAI/ChatGPT as more of a consumer search front end with an underappreciated Codex product; he separately questions whether Cursor has already peaked given competition from Claude Code and Codex [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].

On jobs, Hoffman argues Gen Z should stop "booing" AI and instead position themselves as "generation AI" — the demographic best equipped to make organizations AI-native — pushing back on the narrative that AI is the primary driver of entry-level job losses (citing Goldman Sachs figures of ~16,000 net U.S. jobs/month lost to AI as of April 2026, later ~11,000/month) as partly misattributed "AI washing," blaming pandemic-era over-hiring and remote-work retrenchment instead [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai]. The article also notes Hoffman's departure from Microsoft's board (through year-end) and his new venture, Manas AI, an AI drug-discovery company with Ujjwal Singh and Dr. Siddhartha Mukherjee [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].

## Key claims

- SpaceX went public June 12, 2026, with AI central to its IPO narrative, and shortly after acquired Cursor [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
- All 11 of xAI's original cofounders had left the company by May 2026; Hoffman calls xAI "a complete train wreck" on its "third restart" [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
- The U.S. government issued a June 11, 2026 export-control order forcing Anthropic to pull the Fable and Mythos models from foreign-national access, reportedly after Amazon CEO Andy Jassy raised a jailbreak found in Fable 5 (a flaw Anthropic was already addressing); cybersecurity experts called the response disproportionate [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
- Hoffman: OpenAI was not subject to the same regulatory penalty as Anthropic, which he says undermines any "principled" rationale for the order [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
- Goldman Sachs' AI jobs tracker: ~16,000 net U.S. jobs/month lost to AI as of April 2026, ~11,000/month more recently; graduate unemployment rose from 3.6% (2019) to 5.6% (2026); by mid-2026, 35% of entry-level postings required 3+ years experience and 45% of companies used automated rejection systems [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
- Hoffman (opinion, attributed): entry-level job-market pain is being overattributed to AI ("AI washing") versus pandemic-era over-hiring and remote-work retrenchment [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
- Hoffman is not standing for re-election to Microsoft's board (remains through year-end) after helping broker the LinkedIn acquisition, GitHub purchase, and early Microsoft–OpenAI trust-building [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
- Hoffman's new company Manas AI (with Ujjwal Singh and Dr. Siddhartha Mukherjee) is doing AI-driven small-molecule drug discovery, pitched internally as "an AI drug discovery factory for creating monopolies" [2026-06-24-reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-an-ai-company-xai-complete-trai].
